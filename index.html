TV Voice Remote

Support that stays simple.

TV Voice Remote helps you control a compatible Samsung TV from your iPhone. It connects directly over your local Wi-Fi—there is no account to create.

Getting connected

Keep your iPhone and TV on the same Wi-Fi network. In the app, select your TV, approve the pairing prompt on the TV, and give the remote a name.

Need help?

Open a support request with your TV model, iPhone model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened. Please never include your TV’s IP address, MAC address, pairing token, or any private text.

Get support

Privacy

TV control, paired-TV details, and the text you send remain on your device and local network. Version 1 contains no account system, analytics SDK, ads, or tracking.
