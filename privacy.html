Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026

TV Voice Remote is a free, ad-free remote-control application. This policy explains how version 1 handles information.

Local TV control

The app communicates directly with compatible TVs on your local Wi-Fi network. A TV address, TV name, Wake-on-LAN MAC address, and pairing token are stored only on your iPhone. We do not receive them.

Voice, text, and searches

Voice recognition is performed by Apple services subject to your iPhone settings and permissions. The app sends recognized commands and any text you choose to send directly to your TV. We do not operate servers that receive, store, or analyze speech recordings, search terms, copied text, or typed text.

No tracking or analytics

Version 1 has no account system, analytics SDK, advertising SDK, or cross-app tracking. We do not sell information or use it for advertising.

Retention and deletion

You can remove saved TV information by deleting a saved TV in the app or by deleting the app. For help, use the support request page.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL.
